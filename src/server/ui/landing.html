How it works

One workflow, from a search to a sale

  1. 1

    Discover

    Search for businesses by industry, place and size. Scopely shows who it found, and why a business was left out.

  2. 2

    Prove

    Scopely visits each selected website and records what it observed: the page, the exact link or button, and when it looked.

  3. 3

    Build

    Open a website build or a fix from the evidence, review the draft, and approve the version you want to show.

  4. 4

    Sell

    See who to contact and whether you may, then record your pitch and its outcome. Scopely never sends messages for you.

  5. 5

    Deliver PLANNED

    Tracking delivery is not built yet. Won work is listed, nothing more.

  6. 6

    Verify PLANNED

    Re-checking the original problem after delivery is not built yet. Nothing is marked verified.

Evidence

Every opportunity carries its proof

Observed

What Scopely saw

The page address, the exact element and the time it looked. If it could not see something, it says “unknown” and never guesses.

Why it matters

The problem, in plain words

A button that opens the wrong form, a WhatsApp link with no country code, a booking path that ends nowhere.

What you can sell

A service mapped to it

Each finding points to a fix or a new website. A price appears only when you have set one.

A high-confidence finding is checked again on a fresh visit before it can reach a prospect.

Two kinds of opportunity

A new website, or a fix to the one they have

WEBSITE OPPORTUNITY

Needs a website

Scopely’s own visit found no working website, or the site there cannot do the job. Start a website build from a template and the business’s own details.

Opportunity → Build website

FIX OPPORTUNITY

Needs a fix

A specific, observed problem on a live page, such as a broken contact link. Capture the page as proof, then build the corrected version.

Observed problem → Prove → Build fix

What Scopely does not do

Honest by design

  • It does not send messages. You write and send your own outreach; Scopely records what you did.
  • It does not invent numbers. Revenue, traffic and leads lost stay empty until a real figure is recorded.
  • It does not trust provider data blindly. Names and contact details from data providers stay unverified until you check them.
